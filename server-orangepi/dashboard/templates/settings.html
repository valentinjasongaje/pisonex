Monitoring

Controls how PC screens are streamed to the admin dashboard.

When enabled, opening a PC in fullscreen starts an FFmpeg process on the Windows PC and streams real-time MPEG1 video to the dashboard — giving smooth, high-quality live video with readable text.

When disabled, the fullscreen view falls back to the normal 1-second JPEG snapshot stream — lower quality but works without FFmpeg.
